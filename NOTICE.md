## Shortwave Radio Audio Archive descriptions

The descriptions file holds text from Shortwave Radio Audio Archive item
pages, used only to build the recordist credits shown in the receiver
(`tools/build-recording-credits.py`). That text is published by the Shortwave
Radio Audio Archive under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0
license (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/). It is reproduced
here unmodified, for non-commercial use, with attribution to the Archive and
the contributors named in each entry.

## Archival recordings

The broadcast recordings the receiver plays are **not** in this repository.
They stream from their original hosts, chiefly the Internet Archive. Their
rights belong to their owners. No clearance is implied by this repository.
